How a project runs

No surprises: you always know what’s happening, what it costs, and what you’ll own at the end.

STEP 1

Short discovery call

We talk through the problem in plain English (or 中文). I’ll tell you if it’s a fit, what it involves, and what it should cost — before you commit to anything.

STEP 2

Fixed quote & prototype

You get a written, fixed-scope quote with the timeline in it — usually within 1–2 business days of the call. For AI work, I build a small working prototype on your real documents first (typically 3–5 days), so you judge results — not promises.

STEP 3

Build, test, show

Built in short cycles with demos you can click every few days — most websites and systems are live within 1–2 weeks of starting. Tested properly — for AI systems that includes a measured accuracy set, not just “looks right”.

STEP 4

Handover & support

You receive the code, the documentation and a walkthrough. Optional ongoing maintenance is available if you’d rather not think about it again.

Common questions

Are the case builds past client projects?

No — they’re labelled concept case studies on purpose. They show complete, realistic builds I can deliver with the stack and patterns in my public code, using illustrative business names. For AI work I prototype on your real documents first, so you judge a working result on your own material before committing to a full build.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted fixed-scope after a short discovery call, so you know the full price before work starts. Small, well-defined jobs cost less than open-ended builds — the honest answer depends on scope, which is exactly what the call is for. There’s no charge for the call or the quote.

How long does a typical project take?

Shorter than most people expect, because the scope is fixed up front: a business website is typically live in 5–7 days, a website with a dashboard or a cloud migration in about 7–14 days, most automations in 3–7 days, and an AI chatbot goes from a working prototype in 3–5 days to live in 1–2 weeks. Those ranges assume your content, documents and logins are ready when we start — the fixed quote confirms your exact timeline before any work begins.

Does my project need AI?

Often, no — and I’ll tell you if that’s the case. A well-built website, dashboard or cloud backend solves most business problems on its own. AI earns its place when staff answer the same document-based questions all day, or customers need answers out of hours. It can usually be added later, on top of a solid system, rather than built into everything from day one.

How accurate is an AI chatbot, really?

It depends on your documents — which is why I build an evaluation set from your real questions and measure the answers before launch. The chatbot answers from your documents and cites its sources; when the answer isn’t in your documents, it says so instead of inventing one.

Do I own the code and the data?

Yes. The code, the deployment and your documents are yours. Your documents are used only to build and run your system — they aren’t shared or reused for other clients.

Can you work with my existing website or tools?

Usually, yes. Chatbots can be embedded into an existing site, and automations are built around the tools you already use — forms, email, spreadsheets, accounting software. Tell me what you run on the discovery call and I’ll confirm what’s possible.

Where are you based, and do you work remotely?

I’m based in Sydney and work with clients across Australia and remotely worldwide. I’m available in English and Mandarin.
